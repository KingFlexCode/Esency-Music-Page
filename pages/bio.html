Esency

Artist • Producer • Performer

Born and raised in the Bronx, Esency is a self-made artist-producer known for blending R&B emotion, hip hop charisma, dancehall rhythm, and the warmth of Latin music into a sound that feels raw, cinematic, and unmistakably his.

What began in a high school music class quickly evolved into a full creative identity — producing, writing, and recording records that connected deeply with listeners around the city.

Today, Esency continues to build his world independently, crafting music rooted in emotion, nightlife, culture, confidence, and growth. One record at a time, he's creating something real.
