Born and raised in the Bronx, Esency is a self-made artist-producer whose multicultural influences and melodic instincts shape a sound that moves across genres without ever losing its identity. His music fuses the raw emotion of R&B, the punchline-heavy charisma of hip hop, the rhythm and energy of dancehall, and the warmth of Latin music — reflecting the diverse soundtrack of his upbringing.

Esency’s journey began in high school, where a music class introduced him to FL Studio and sparked his passion for production. What started as beat-making quickly evolved into songwriting after he began recording reference vocals for artists who doubted a producer could also write. The reaction to those early demos revealed something bigger: a voice that could carry stories, melodies, and emotions in a way that resonated far beyond the studio.

College added another layer to his artistry. As a DJ, Esency was exposed to reggae, dancehall, and the Caribbean sounds influencing New York’s underground scene. That fusion inspired “She Want It,” a dancehall-flavored record that became his first viral moment, building a core fanbase that still follows him today. He continued expanding his catalog with records like So What, Sprung, Bronx Whine, and full projects like EXE and Nada Personal, showcasing his versatility as a producer, writer, and performer.

Over the years, Esency has built momentum as an independent artist — earning over 100,000 streams on a single track, landing radio placements, and performing at major events including a dancehall/afrobeats festival in Brooklyn, a college show where he gifted 200 shirts and mixtapes, and the iconic SOB’s. Even more meaningful are the moments when young fans approach him not knowing he’s the face behind the music they’ve been hearing in schools, parties, and social circles.

What sets Esency apart is his creative workflow: he improvises, writes on the spot, and builds records directly from feeling. As a producer, he crafts beats that perfectly match his vocal tone and melodic instincts. As an artist, he channels themes of love, nightlife, confidence, heartbreak, and growth — all while remaining authentic, humorous, and grounded.

Looking ahead, Esency plans to continue growing independently while exploring the musical paths that resonate most, whether dancehall, Latin, or melodic hip hop. He doesn’t chase gimmicks; he lets the people guide the direction. One record at a time, he’s building a world where listeners feel connected, inspired, and part of something real.
