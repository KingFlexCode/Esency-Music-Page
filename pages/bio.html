Esency

Artist • Producer • Songwriter

Artist Bio

Esency is a Bronx-born artist-producer whose multicultural influences and melodic instincts shape a sound that blends R&B, hip hop, dancehall, and Latin rhythms into something emotionally raw and uniquely personal.

His journey began with production in high school, evolved through songwriting in college, and eventually grew into a full creative identity that connected deeply with listeners through authentic stories and genre-blending energy.

Continuing to work as an independent artist, Esency draws on real experiences — love, nightlife, heartbreak, confidence, growth — to create a soundscape that feels honest, vibrant, and emotionally charged.
