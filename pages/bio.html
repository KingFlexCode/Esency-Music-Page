Where Dancehall, Latin & R&B Collide

Born and raised in the Bronx, Esency is a self-made artist-producer whose multicultural influences and melodic instincts shape a sound that moves across genres without ever losing its identity. His music blends the emotion of R&B, the charisma of hip hop, the rhythm of dancehall, and the warmth of Latin music.

Esency’s journey began in high school, where FL Studio sparked his passion for production. What started as beat-making evolved into songwriting when he realized his voice carried emotion that connected with listeners. This creative spark pushed him beyond producing — toward becoming a full artist.

While DJ'ing in college, he discovered reggae, dancehall, and Caribbean-inspired rhythms that shaped his breakout song “She Want It.” Since then, he has released records like So What, Sprung, Bronx Whine, and full projects like EXE and Nada Personal, all produced and written by him.

His journey as an independent artist includes surpassing 100k streams on a single track, landing radio placements, performing at festivals, and taking the stage at SOB’s — a venue visited by hip-hop legends. Fans often tell him they’ve been listening to his music long before realizing he was the artist behind it.

Known for improvising and creating music from pure emotion, Esency builds beats and lyrics simultaneously. His themes revolve around confidence, nightlife, love, heartbreak, and growth. His humor, hustle, and authenticity make listeners feel like they’re growing with him.

As he looks ahead, Esency plans to expand his audience while staying true to his roots — letting the fans guide the journey organically. Each record builds a world where listeners feel connected, inspired, and part of something real.
